# Pharma Drug Sales Analysis (Power BI)

An end-to-end Power BI sales analysis project exploring pharmaceutical drug sales trends across daily, hourly, weekly, monthly, and yearly granularities, with a strong focus on category performance and inventory/business recommendations.

---

## Project Structure
- pharma_drug_sales_project.pbix — Interactive Power BI report and data model
- salesdaily — Daily drug category sales dataset
- saleshourly — Hourly drug category sales dataset
- salesweekly — Weekly drug category sales dataset
- salesmonthly — Monthly drug category sales dataset

---

## Key KPIs & Metrics
- Total Sales: 126.59K
- Average Sales: 15.82K
- Weekly Sales Contribution by N02BE: 49.4%
- Total Sales by N02BE: 63K
- Drug Categories Analyzed: M01AB, M01AE, N02BA, N02BE, N05B, N05C, R03, R06

---

## Key Insights
- N02BE Leads Across Views: N02BE accounts for 49.48% of daily sales and dominates weekly and yearly totals (such as 1,829.96 out of 3,374.06 in Week 5 and 13,435.83 in 2016).
- Top Two Concentration: N02BE (49.48%) and N05B (14.66%) together drive 64.14% of daily sales.
- Weekly & Yearly Peaks: Weekly sales peak in Week 5 (3,374.06), while yearly sales rise from 2014 to a peak in 2016 before fluctuating across 2017–2019.
- Long-Tail Categories: N05C contributes the smallest share (0.98% daily; 207 in 2016), while mid-tier categories range between 4.77% and 9.12% daily.

---

## Recommendations
- Prioritize Core Inventory: Maintain strong safety stock and reliable supply chains for high-volume categories like N02BE and N05B to prevent stock-outs.
- Prepare for Peak Demand: Align stock levels ahead of peak weekly demand (around Weeks 4–6) and review post-2016 yearly fluctuations for better forecasting.
- Optimize Low-Share Drugs: Keep inventory lean for low-contributing categories such as N05C to minimize holding costs and avoid dead stock.
